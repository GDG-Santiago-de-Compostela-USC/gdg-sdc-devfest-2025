# DevFest Santiago de Compostela 2026

Direction approved in the conversation: a bright Google visual language inspired by Gemini Enterprise, grounded in the GDG community and the supplied four-color GDG symbol.

## Foundation

- White `#ffffff` surfaces, `#202124` text, `#5f6368` secondary text.
- Blue `#1a73e8` actions, `#1558b0` hover, visible blue keyboard focus.
- GDG mark: blue `#4285f4`, red `#ea4335`, yellow `#fbbc04`, green `#0f9d58`.
- Google Sans Display, loaded locally; regular headings and readable body copy.
- Content width 1180px, generous section spacing, left alignment.
- Pill-shaped primary actions; photographs and large visual surfaces have their own radius and hierarchy.

## Composition

The hero pairs the event name, date, location and registration with a large GDG mark. React Bits Magnet is the single expressive interaction: the mark follows the pointer slightly, returning naturally to rest. Touch and reduced motion retain a static mark.

Home previews use real public Sessionize data. The agenda is a compact list, portraits introduce the people, sponsor logos carry their own identity, and practical information uses direct links and native disclosures. Preserve confirmed content and do not invent attendance figures, schedules or event photographs.

## Quality

Keep mobile layouts fluid down to 320px, preserve accessible focus and the native mobile dialog, maintain text contrast, and avoid hiding essential content behind animation. The 2025 static archive remains its original edition.
